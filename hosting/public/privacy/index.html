Privacy Policy

Tap & Dodge · Last updated: 15 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Tap & Dodge (“the App”) handles information when you play on Android. Contact: navaparameetdev@gmail.com

1) Local progress

Guest and signed-in play store campaign progress (levels, coins, scores, skins, streak) in on-device preferences on your phone.

2) Google account (optional)

If you choose Continue with Google or Save My Progress, we use Firebase Authentication with Google Sign-In. We receive a Firebase user id and may receive your Google display name, email, and profile photo URL from Google. We use these to identify your account and show your name in Account settings.

3) Cloud save

Signed-in progress is stored in Google Cloud Firestore under your Firebase user id so you can restore on another device. Guest progress is not uploaded to a shared public document.

4) Ads

Internet is required for gameplay ads. We show rewarded, interstitial, and app-open ads via Google AdMob. AdMob and partners may process advertising ID, IP/device/network signals, and interaction data to serve and measure ads.

5) Consent

In the EEA/UK and similar regions we use Google’s UMP consent message before personalized ads when required. You can also change device ad settings in system settings.

6) Account deletion

Signed-in players can delete their Tap & Dodge account data in Account → Delete Account & Cloud Save. That removes the cloud save and the Firebase Auth link for this app. It does not delete your Google Account. You may also email navaparameetdev@gmail.com to request deletion.

7) Children

This game is a general-audience casual arcade title and is not directed at children under 13. Do not use the App if you are under 13 without a parent/guardian. Parents should supervise play and device ad settings.

8) Selling data

We do not sell your personal information. Third-party SDKs (AdMob, Firebase, Google Sign-In) process data under their own policies as needed to provide ads and account features.

9) Changes

We may update this policy when features change. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we publish a revision.

10) Contact

Questions or privacy requests: navaparameetdev@gmail.com
